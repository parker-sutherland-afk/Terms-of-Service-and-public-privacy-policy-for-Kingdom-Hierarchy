Kingdom Hierarchy operates on a strict zero-data-collection basis:

  • No Personal Data Storage: We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information on our own databases or servers.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: We do not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics cookies, or behavioural profiling tools.

2. Authentication & Third-Party Services

To allow you to securely log in without us storing your passwords or personal records, we rely on trusted third-party infrastructure providers:

Kingdom Hierarchy's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
